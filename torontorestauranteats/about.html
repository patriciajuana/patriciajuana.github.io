Our story

About Toronto Restaurant Eats

Founded by Jonathan & Logan

Toronto Restaurant Eats was founded by Jonathan and Logan, two passionate food lovers with a shared appreciation for Toronto's incredible culinary scene. As co-owners of the brand, they created Toronto Restaurant Eats to help locals and visitors discover restaurants that deliver memorable dining experiences.

From neighbourhood gems and family-run eateries to upscale dining destinations and exciting new openings, their goal is to showcase the restaurants that make Toronto one of Canada's most diverse and exciting food cities.

Why we explore

Every great meal
tells a story.

At Toronto Restaurant Eats, we believe that every great meal tells a story. Our mission is to explore Toronto restaurants across every cuisine, neighbourhood, and price point while providing honest insights that help readers find the best places to eat in Toronto.

Whether you're planning a special occasion, searching for your next favourite brunch spot, or simply looking for somewhere new to dine, we're here to inspire your next meal while supporting the hardworking chefs, restaurateurs, and local businesses that make Toronto's food scene thrive.

Good food. Shared stories.

Let's work together

Partner With Toronto Restaurant Eats

Our mission is to celebrate Toronto's incredible restaurant community while helping great businesses reach more people. In addition to independently discovering restaurants throughout the city, we also collaborate with restaurant owners through sponsored content opportunities.

If you'd like Toronto Restaurant Eats to visit your restaurant and create a professionally written sponsored feature, we'd love to connect. We take the time to showcase what makes your restaurant special while maintaining content that's engaging, informative, and valuable to our readers.

Get in touch with Jonathan and Logan to discuss sponsorship opportunities and learn how we can help introduce your restaurant to more diners.
