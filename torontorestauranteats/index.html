Little Italy · Italian · $$

Cedar Lane Trattoria

We came for a quick pasta dinner and ended up staying all evening. The rigatoni had a lovely bite, the tomato sauce was rich without feeling heavy, and the warm focaccia was gone almost as soon as it arrived.

Our server made thoughtful wine suggestions and never rushed us. It gets a little loud later on, but for a relaxed dinner with friends, this is exactly the kind of neighbourhood spot I would come back to.

Best Toronto Restaurants FAQ

What are the best restaurants in Toronto?

The best Toronto restaurants range from internationally recognized fine dining establishments to hidden local favourites serving authentic cuisine from around the world. Our guides showcase restaurants across different cuisines, budgets, and neighbourhoods so you can find the right dining experience.

Where are the best places to eat in Toronto?

Some of the best places to eat in Toronto can be found throughout neighbourhoods like Downtown, Yorkville, King West, Queen West, Kensington Market, The Annex, Leslieville, North York, and Scarborough. We regularly feature restaurants across the city.

Where can I find good places to eat in downtown Toronto?

Downtown Toronto offers everything from upscale restaurants and rooftop patios to casual cafés, quick lunch spots, and late-night eateries. Our downtown restaurant guides make it easy to discover great options for every occasion.

Do you feature healthy places to eat in Toronto?

Yes. From fresh salads and protein-focused meals to vegan restaurants and nutritious cafés, we highlight healthy places to eat in Toronto for readers looking for balanced dining options.
